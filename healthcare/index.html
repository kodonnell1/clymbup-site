Where it leaks

Where practices lose time and revenue

No-shows and late cancellations

An empty slot can't be sold again once the hour has passed. Without a reminder that lets patients confirm or reschedule, you find out when the chair is already empty.

Calls nobody can pick up

Patients call while the front desk is checking someone in, during lunch, and after hours. Many hang up instead of leaving a voicemail and call the next practice.

Patients who quietly drift away

Overdue cleanings, missed annual visits and follow-ups that never got booked. They're already your patients, and often nobody reaches out.

Questions

Common questions

How do you handle HIPAA and patient information?
Before we handle any patient information, we sign a business associate agreement (BAA) with your practice, and we only use vendors that will sign one with us. Your written report spells out which parts of a build touch patient information and which don't.
Will the after-hours texting line give medical advice?
No. It handles the practical questions: hours, directions, rescheduling, and passing refill or records requests to your staff. Anything clinical goes to your staff, and anyone describing an emergency is told to call 911.
Does it work with the scheduling system we already use?
Usually. We start with what you already use. If your system has a way to connect, we build on it. If it doesn't, your report says so plainly and lays out the options.
Do patients need to download an app?
No. Reminders, confirmations and the texting line all work by regular text message, and the phone receptionist answers a regular phone call.
Can it talk to patients in Spanish?
Yes. The phone receptionist speaks English and Spanish, and patient texts can go out in the patient's preferred language.
