Before you call

Questions people ask first

Is the call actually free?
Yes. No credit card, no obligation. We use it to figure out — honestly — whether there's a real automation opportunity in your business. Most calls end with either a clear next step or an honest "this probably isn't worth it yet."
What's the $999 Audit for, then?
The free call is a quick gut-check. The Audit is the real work: a 45-minute discovery call with Kevin personally, plus a 10–15 page written report with prioritized recommendations. It only gets offered when the free call suggests it's actually worth doing. Full details →
How much does it cost to actually build the automation?
Value-based, not a flat rate — priced against what we can show it actually saves your business, one time to set it up plus a smaller amount to maintain it. If we can't point to a real, provable number, we won't build it and we won't charge you for it. Either way, the $999 you paid for the Audit is credited toward the build — you're never paying twice.
What kinds of businesses is this for?
Local service and appointment-based businesses — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, healthcare and dental practices, salons, auto repair. If the owner or team is doing repetitive manual work that could be automated, it's probably worth a call.
