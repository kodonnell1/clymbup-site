Yes. No credit card, no obligation. We use it to figure out, honestly, whether there's a real automation opportunity in your business. Most conversations end with either a clear next step or an honest "this probably isn't worth it yet."
What if I'm not sure where to start?
That's normal, most owners aren't sure either. Call and tell us what's eating your time, or take the free assessment first if you'd rather start there. Either way, we'll tell you the one thing worth fixing first.

Value-based, not a flat rate. It's priced against what we can actually show it saves your business: one cost to set it up, plus a smaller amount to maintain it. If we can't point to a real, provable number, we won't build it, and we won't charge you for it.
