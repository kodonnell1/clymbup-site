Yes. No credit card, no obligation. We use it to figure out — honestly — whether there's a real automation opportunity in your business. Most conversations end with either a clear next step or an honest "this probably isn't worth it yet."
What if I'm not sure where to start?
That's normal — most owners aren't. Call and tell us what's eating your time, or take the free assessment first if you'd rather start there. Either way, we'll point at the specific thing worth fixing first.
