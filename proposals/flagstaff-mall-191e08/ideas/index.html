ProposalIdeas for later

Shopping centers are adding AI helpers for shoppers and staff. Fashion Island in Newport Beach answers shopper questions by text, and Simon malls offer a concierge bot. Features like these used to be big-mall extras and are becoming standard. Once your new site is running, any of the ideas below can be added to it.

These are optional add-ons and not part of the quote on the first page. Each would be priced when you're ready.

Easiest to add first

A shopper assistant, by chat and text

A chat box on the website, and a phone number shoppers can text, that answer common questions any time of day. It reads from the same store directory, hours and events your team already keeps current, so its answers stay up to date without extra work.

Is there a shoe store in the mall?
Yes. Here are the stores that sell shoes, with links to each one's page and hours.
What time do you close on Sunday?
Here are this Sunday's mall hours. A few stores keep their own hours; tell me which one you're heading to and I'll check it.

It could be ready on launch day. Questions it can't answer go to your team.

For your staffLess time updating the site

Text or call in your updates

Text a number with "Add the trick-or-treat event, Oct 31, 4 to 7 PM", or call and say it. The assistant drafts the change and texts back a preview; reply YES to publish.

Only approved staff phone numbers can make changes, and every change can be undone.

Event flyer to event page

Text or upload a photo of an event flyer. The details are read off the flyer and turned into an event page for you to approve.

No retyping dates, times and descriptions.

Holiday hours everywhere at once

Change special hours once, and they update on the website, your Google listing and the shopper assistant together.

Shoppers stop finding the wrong hours on Google.

New events drafted as social posts

Each new event becomes a ready-to-approve Facebook and Instagram post with the event image.

Events get promoted without someone writing every post.

For management and leasingKnow what shoppers want

"What shoppers are looking for" report

A monthly summary of what people search for and ask the assistant, including stores and services the mall doesn't have yet.

Real demand data to bring to leasing conversations.

Found correctly by AI search

More shoppers ask Google, ChatGPT and other assistants what stores a mall has. Clean store and event data on the site helps them answer correctly.

The answer people get matches your actual directory.

Spanish version

The website and shopper assistant offered in Spanish, translated automatically and kept in step with every update.

Welcomes more of the shoppers you already serve.
