Before you book

Questions people ask first

What kinds of businesses is this for?
Any business where the owner or team is doing repetitive manual work that could be automated. We specialize in home service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, carpet cleaning — but the audit works for any small business running lean.
What if I'm not tech-savvy?
That's exactly who this is for. We explain everything in plain English and only recommend tools you'll actually use. You don't need to know what an API is. If something requires technical setup, we'll tell you exactly how to get it done — or offer to do it for you.
What kinds of tools will you recommend?
Whatever fits your situation. That might be a no-code automation platform like Zapier or Make, a scheduling app you're underusing, or something built specifically for your industry. We're tool-agnostic — we don't take commissions and we don't have brand deals.
Do I have to implement it myself?
No. The report gives you everything you need to implement it yourself, hand it to a team member, or bring in a freelancer. If you want us to handle implementation, we offer that separately — but there's no pressure and it's never part of the audit pitch.
How is this different from a free consultation?
A free consultation gives you 30 minutes of general conversation. The Automation Audit gives you a 45-minute deep dive into your specific business, a 10–15 page written report with concrete recommendations, and a second call to walk through it together. It's a structured engagement with a real deliverable — not a discovery call with a sales pitch at the end.
