The cost of voicemail

What a missed call costs an electrician

The calls you miss aren't all dead outlets. A panel upgrade often runs $2,000 to $4,000, and EV charger installs keep coming as more people in the valley buy electric cars.

Say you miss 4 calls a week, and 1 in 4 was a real job at a $500 ticket. That's roughly $2,000 a month. Miss a single panel upgrade and you've lost most of that in one call.

How it works

How an electrical call gets handled

1

Safety comes first

If the caller mentions sparks, smoke or a burning smell, the AI tells them to call 911 if there's any danger and to shut off that breaker if it's safe, then marks the call urgent.

2

Then it qualifies the job

Is it the whole house or one circuit? Is it a repair or an install, like a panel upgrade, an EV charger or new lighting? How old is the home? Then the address and best callback number.

3

You get the job, not just a name

The text you get says what the job is, how urgent it is and what the caller told it, so you can quote on the callback instead of starting from scratch. You can text back from the business number.

Anyone who hangs up before the AI answers gets a text back within seconds, so the lead isn't lost.

Reputation

People let you into their panel because they trust you

Electrical work makes homeowners nervous. Before they call, they check that you're licensed and that other people trust you. ClymbUp watches your Google and Yelp reviews every day, flags a bad one right away with a drafted reply, and sends review requests after each job so your reputation keeps up with your work.

Questions

Common questions

How does it handle dangerous calls, like sparks or a burning smell?
It tells the caller to call 911 if there's any danger, to turn off that breaker if it's safe to do so, and marks the call urgent so it reaches you immediately.
Can it qualify panel upgrade and EV charger leads?
Yes. It asks about the home's age, the current panel if they know it, and for chargers, the car and where it would go. You get that in the summary so you can price the job on your first call back.
Can it tell callers I'm licensed?
Yes. We set it up with your license number and anything else callers should know, like whether you're insured and which cities you cover.
Do I have to change my phone number?
No. Your phone rings first, and only unanswered calls go to the AI. Your number stays the same everywhere.
