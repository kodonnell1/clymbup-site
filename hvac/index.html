The cost of voicemail

What the summer rush costs you

From June to September, the phone rings faster than any office can keep up with. Every call that goes unanswered during a heat wave is someone dialing the next company within a minute.

Say you miss 8 calls a week at peak, and 1 in 4 was a repair at a $400 ticket. That's roughly $3,400 a month in the busiest months. And many of your biggest jobs start as a repair call: a full system replacement can run $8,000 or more.

How it works

How an HVAC call gets handled

1

No busy signal, ever

The AI can take several calls at once, so a heat-wave morning doesn't turn into a wall of voicemails. Your phone still rings first for the calls you can take.

2

It asks the questions that set priority

Is the system not cooling at all, or just weak? What's the temperature inside? Is anyone at home elderly, very young, or medically at risk? Are they on a maintenance plan? Then the address and callback number.

3

Emergencies reach you first

No-cool calls with someone at risk are marked urgent in the text you get, above the tune-up requests. You can reply to the customer by text from the business number.

Callers who hang up before the AI picks up get a text back right away, which matters most in July, when they're calling down a list.

Reputation

Summer is when your reputation is made

Homeowners compare HVAC companies on reviews before they let anyone into their attic, and summer is when the most reviews get written, good and bad. ClymbUp watches your Google and Yelp reviews every day, alerts you right away to a bad one with a drafted reply, and sends review requests after each job so the good summer work actually shows up online.

Questions

Common questions

What happens during the summer rush?
The AI answers as many calls at once as come in, so callers never hit a busy signal or voicemail. You get each one as a text summary, with the urgent ones clearly marked.
What if a caller has no AC and someone vulnerable at home?
It asks, and if there's an elderly person, a baby or someone with a medical condition in the house, the call is marked urgent and reaches you immediately. If anyone is in danger, it tells the caller to call 911.
Can it book tune-ups and maintenance visits?
It collects the details and the customer's preferred times. If you want it to book straight onto your calendar, we can connect it to the scheduling software you already use.
Do I have to change my phone number?
No. You keep your number, and calls only forward to the AI when you or your office don't answer.
