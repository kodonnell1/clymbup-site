Usage rules

✓ Do

  • Use on dark backgrounds (#0b1220 or similar)
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "C"
  • Use SVG for web — it scales perfectly
  • Use the icon mark alone at small sizes (<120px wide)

✗ Don't

  • Stretch or distort the logo
  • Place on busy photo backgrounds without a dark overlay
  • Use on pure white without a dark card backing
  • Change the gradient colors

06 — Voice & Tone

Direct over corporate.

Say "Stop losing leads to voicemail" not "Optimize your inbound lead capture pipeline." Small business owners speak plainly. So do we.

Small business context, always.

The audience is owner-operators of home service companies — HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, pest control. Every benefit should be relatable to their day, not to enterprise SaaS buyers.

Numbers over adjectives.

Not "significantly faster response times" — say "answered in under 2 seconds, 24/7." Specificity builds trust.

No AI buzzwords.

Avoid "leveraging cutting-edge AI," "next-gen," "revolutionary," "seamless." These signal generic content. Use functional descriptions instead.

First person is Kevin.

On the About page and in emails, the voice is Kevin's — a real person, not a brand committee. Use "I" and "we" deliberately.

Calls to action are specific.

"Book a 15-minute strategy call" beats "Get started." "Call our AI agent — (760) 565-5905" beats "Try a demo." Name the action and the duration or destination.
